## What is Civic technology and how can smart cities benefit from it?##

#Civic technology is a method of using technology to solve governmental issues
# through collaboration and between agencies and the public. You can achieve this
#by analyzing open data, creating website, applications and other tools that help
#citizens understand the root of the issues and most importantly, provide their
#input/opinion. 


#Smart cities benefit from civic technology because it allows local governments 
#to collect information and respond to community needs more effectively. 
#For example, similarly as the 311 Chicago application, residents can use mobile
#applications to report potholes, broken streetlights, or other infrastructure
#problems.This information can then be sent directly to city departments for action.

#From the financial aspect, civic technology also increases transparency. 
#Governments can publish their financial statements, budgets, performance measures
#and public data online so residents can better understand how public resources 
#are being used. This can improve the relationship and trust between the citizens 
#and government officials.

#Another way citizens may feel engaged and part of something, is by providing their
#opinion/input . Residents can participate in surveys, public meeting and 
#community discussions through online platforms. This allows governments to gather
#feedback from more people than would be possible through traditional meetings. 

#Smart cities can also use civic technology together with sensors and data analytic
#to improve transportation, public safety, energy use and environmental sustainability. 
#By using data to identify problems and make better decisions cities can provide 
#services more efficiently and improve quality of life for everyone.

#Civic technology provides the avenue and tools to collaborate, create and make 
#positive impact for society.
